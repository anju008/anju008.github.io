Project overview

This portfolio simulation was rebuilt from an earlier e-commerce dashboard into a more role-relevant B2B CRM model. The goal was to show how I would think about pipeline health, lead quality, email automation, content performance, and regional optimization for a mobility platform.

The work is based on simulated data and designed to reflect the kind of thinking a CRM & Marketing Automation Intern would bring to a business focused on lead lifecycle, segmentation, and conversion efficiency.

What this project taught me

  • Data structure matters: clean stage definitions and clear field logic make reporting trustworthy.
  • Lead scoring should predict conversion: intent signals matter more than activity alone.
  • Dashboarding is not the end goal: every insight needs a recommended next action.
  • CRM thinking is cross-functional: marketing, sales, and content all need to work from the same funnel logic.

Next phase

The next step is to move from reporting into optimization: workflow maps, more tailored nurture journeys, stronger case study assets, and a more deliberate content calendar that supports lead consistency across the year.
